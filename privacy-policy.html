Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Health Archive" application ("we", "us", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you connect your WHOOP account through the WHOOP Developer Platform.

1. Data We Access

When you authorize the App via OAuth 2.0, we request access to the following WHOOP data categories, solely for your personal health tracking purposes:

  • Recovery data — recovery scores, Heart Rate Variability (HRV), resting heart rate
  • Sleep data — sleep performance, sleep stage durations
  • Cycle data — daily Strain, average heart rate per physiological cycle
  • Workout data — activity Strain, average heart rate during workouts
  • Body measurement data — height, weight, maximum heart rate
  • Profile data — name and email address

2. How We Use Your Data

Your WHOOP data is used exclusively for:

  • Personal health monitoring and trend analysis
  • Generating personal health reports and visualizations
  • Long-term archiving of your personal health metrics

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or any commercial purpose.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored locally on your personal device(s) only.
  • Access tokens are encrypted and stored securely; they are never shared or exposed publicly.
  • The Client Secret used for authentication is kept server-side and is never exposed in any client application.
  • Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or lease your personal data to any third party. Your data is for your personal use only.

5. Data Retention

Your WHOOP data is retained for as long as you maintain your connection to the App. You may revoke access at any time through the WHOOP app or by contacting us, after which we will delete all stored data within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

  • Access — You can view all data collected from your WHOOP account at any time.
  • Revoke Access — You can disconnect the App from your WHOOP account at any time via WHOOP's settings.
  • Deletion — You can request deletion of your stored data at any time.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the App administrator.
